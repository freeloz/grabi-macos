Hi, I'm Grabi

Thanks for trying the beta. Installing takes two minutes — and the first time, macOS will ask for one extra step. Here's everything, no drama.

Why does macOS show a warning when you open it? Grabi is in beta and doesn't have Apple's certification yet (it will come with the public release). The warning is normal and expected: macOS is only asking you to confirm that you trust us. Nothing leaves your Mac — Grabi doesn't use the internet.

1 · Install

1

Open the Grabi-….dmg file you downloaded.

2

In the window that appears, drag Grabi to the Applications folder (the arrow shows you where). You can now eject the disk.

2 · The first launch (the extra step)

1

Open Grabi from Applications. macOS will show a warning like "Apple could not verify that "Grabi" is free of malware" and won't open it. Click OK / Done — do not choose "Move to Trash".

2

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section: you'll see ""Grabi" was blocked to protect your Mac". Click Open Anyway.

3

macOS will ask for your password or Touch ID and one last confirmation: Open Anyway. This happens only once — from here on, Grabi opens normally.

Plan B — macOS says "Grabi is damaged" or nothing happens. On some recent macOS versions the warning doesn't offer "Open Anyway". Grabi is fine — it's the same beta check. Open Terminal (⌘Space, type "Terminal"), paste this line, press Enter, and open Grabi again:

xattr -cr /Applications/Grabi.app

It removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark — nothing else. Make sure Grabi is in Applications first (step 1·2), not running from inside the DMG.

3 · The recording permissions

1

Grabi lives in the menu bar (the dot with a ring, at the top right) — it doesn't appear in the Dock. When you open it, it greets you and guides you.

2

Accept the Camera and Microphone permissions when macOS asks for them.

3

For Screen Recording, macOS takes you to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording: turn on Grabi and open it again. It's only for recording — nothing leaves your Mac.

And to uninstall?

Drag Grabi from Applications to the Trash. Your recordings stay with you in Movies → Recordings (or the folder you chose in Settings). If you also want to delete the preferences: ~/Library/Preferences/com.ivan.recordapp.plist.
