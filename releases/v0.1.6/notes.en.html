Grabi 0.1.6

  • Grabi now has a window. A normal Mac app: an icon in the Dock, menus at the top, and one window with everything — record, your recordings and settings. The menu bar item stays for recording without opening the window.
  • Your recordings, in the app. Thumbnails, duration and size; play, show in Finder, copy or move to the Trash without leaving Grabi.
  • Much lighter. Fixed a bug that pinned the CPU and made Grabi feel stuck, and the preview no longer runs at full quality just because the window is open.
  • Always where you left it. If the window had been saved on a display you no longer have, it could open off-screen and look like the app never launched.
