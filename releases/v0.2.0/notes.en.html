Grabi 0.2.0

  • Leaving the Record tab really stops everything. Camera, screen and microphone are released the moment you switch to Recordings or Settings — no camera light, no sharing indicator.
  • Pick your camera and your microphone. External webcams, Continuity Camera and USB mics all show up; if one gets unplugged Grabi falls back to the system default instead of failing.
  • Grabi speaks your language, even if your Mac doesn't. A language picker in Settings, applied right away.
  • Live level meters for the microphone and for the system audio.
  • "Change…" now redraws the region when you are recording a region, instead of opening the display picker.
  • Opening the window from the menu bar puts the panel away, and the preview no longer fails to start on some launches.
